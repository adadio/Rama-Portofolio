IOT THESIS CASE STUDY

Automatic Catfish Feeding System

Informatics Thesis Project | Institut Bisnis Muhammadiyah Bekasi

Objective: Eliminate overfeeding and manual feeding labor in Sangkuriang catfish farming through automated weight-measured feed dispensing.

System Architecture: Integrated an ESP32 microcontroller with an HX711 load-cell amplifier for real-time feed weight feedback. Configured servo actuators to open dispensing gates until exact target weight was registered, sending telemetry to Blynk cloud interface.

Key Engineering Learnings: Calibration of analog load sensors, hardware noise filtering, and micro-controller WiFi reconnect fault tolerance.
